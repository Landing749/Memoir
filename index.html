Polaroids and collages you can keep

The Studio draws real PNG files on your device from your saved memories. Your original photo is never changed.

  • Polaroid cards with a caption, date, paper color and doodles
  • Collages of 2 to 12 photos, with several layouts
  • Adjustable spacing, margins and background
  • Saved to your own storage only when you export

Private by design

  • No AI.No image recognition, no generated text.
  • No cloud.No servers, sync or online backup.
  • No accounts.Open it and start. Nothing to sign up for.
  • No tracking.No analytics, ads or crash uploads.
  • No internet.The app doesn’t ask for the permission at all.

Photos and notes are stored in the app’s private storage on your phone. A file you export or share yourself is your copy, outside the app. Android itself and your device backups are beyond Memoir’s control.

Questions

Does Memoir need an internet connection?

No. Prompts, search, Polaroids and collages all run on the device.

Where are my photos kept?

Imported and captured photos are copied into the app’s own storage, so they stay available after a restart without broad photo-library access.

How are the prompts chosen?

A person wrote them and they ship inside the app. Simple local rules, such as skipping recently shown prompts, pick the next one. Memoir doesn’t guess your mood or read your photos.

What happens if I delete everything?

“Delete all data” removes the app’s memories and files after you confirm. Files you exported to shared storage are not touched.

Can I back up or move my memories?

You can export Polaroids and collages as PNG files. A full backup feature is not part of the current scope.
